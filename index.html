How it works

Depth: Starting from depth = 1 at the trunk, every branch splits recursively into smaller left and right child branches until reaching the chosen depth.

Base case: When depth === maxDepth, recursion stops and blooms into a glowing leaf tip. Children only start growing after their parent finishes.

Branch to sound: The trunk plays the low root anchor; deeper branches climb up the pentatonic scale quietly, pan left/right by horizontal position, and harmonize with the pad chord on the beat grid.
